Artificial Intelligence

Technology in Action

Aritificial Intelligence (AI) is widely used in different areas of modern society. It helps people complete tasks, analyse information, make decision and improve efficiency.


Education

Resources: magnific.com

AI can support students and teachers in education. AI-powered learning tools can provide personalised exercises, explain difficult topics and give feedback. Teachers can also yse AI to prepare learning materials and analyse students' performance.

Example:

  • AI learning assistants
  • Personalised learning platforms
  • Automated feedback


Business

Resources: magnific.com

AI is used by businesses to improve productivity and customer service. AI chatbots can answer customer questions, while AI can analyse business data and help companies identify trends. It can also automate repetitive tasks.

Example:

  • Customer service chatbots
  • Data analysis
  • Fraud detection


Healthcare

Resources: magnific.com

AI can support healthcare professionals by analysing medical information and helping detect certain diseases. It can also assist with patient monitoring and medical research.

Example:

  • Medical image analysis
  • Patient monitoring
  • Disease detection


Government

Resources: magnific.com

Government can use AI to analyse large amounts of information and improve public services. AI can also help detect unusual activities, manage traffic and support government decision-making.

Example:

  • Traffic management
  • Public service chatbots
  • Data analysis


Daily Life

AI is also part of everyday life. People use AI through smartphones, search engines, navigation apps and recommendation systems. These technologies can provide personalised information and make daily tasks more convinient.

Example:

  • Voice assistants
  • Google Maps route suggestions
  • Smartphone AI features
