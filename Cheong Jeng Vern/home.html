Artificial Intelligence


Introduction

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that allows computers and machines to perfom tasks that usually require human intelligence. Nowadays, AI is becoming more common in our daily lives and workplaces. It can help people complete tasks, solve problems, analyse information and make decisions. This website introduces the basic ideaas of AI and explains how it is used in different areas.


Overview

This website provides an overview of Artificial Intelligence (AI). It covers the definition of AI, how AI works, its benefits and limitations, and its applications in different areas, It also introduces some career opportunities related to AI and explains why learning about AI is important for the future.
