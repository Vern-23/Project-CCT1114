Artificial Intelligence

Definition

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to the ability of computers or machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.These tasks can include learning, problem-solving, understanding language, recognising images and making decisions. AI uses data and computer algorithms to perform these tasks.

Workplace Applications

Fast Problem Solving AI can help people complete tasks faster and improve work efficiency.
Works 24/7 AI systems can operate continuously without needing breaks, depending on the system and application.
Reduced Human Error AI can operate continuously without needing breaks, depending on the system and application.
Help with Decision-Making AI can analyse large amounts of data and provide useful information to support decision-making.
Save Times and Costs AI can reduce the time and resources needed to complete them.

Limitations/Challenges

  • High Cost

    • >Developing and maintaining AI systems can be expensive.

  • Data Dependency

    • >AI needs accurate and sufficient data to produce reliable results.

  • Privacy and Security

    • >Using large amounts of data can create privacy and security concerns.

  • Bias and Unfair Results

    • >AI may produce biased results if the data used to train it contains bias.

  • Lack of Human understanding

    • >AI may have difficulty understanding human emotions, context and complex situations.
